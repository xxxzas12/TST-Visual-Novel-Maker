# TSTVN v1.0.19

UI/UX polish pass: layout, spacing, colors, visual hierarchy and usability only. No new features, no
project-format, export or plugin changes.

How it was done: screenshots of every view (Welcome, Scenes, Story Flow, Assets, Characters,
Variables, Themes, Project, Export, Backups, Application Settings, light mode) were taken before and
after, and the problems below were picked from them.

## Added
- Nothing new functionally.
- Getting-started checklist:
  - a thin progress bar;
  - the next step is highlighted;
  - when folded, the bar shows "🎓 Next: ‹step›".

## Changed
- **Getting-started checklist (onboarding):**
  - Narrower and more compact (it used to cover inspector fields and buttons on every screen).
  - The whole header folds/unfolds it, and the folded state is remembered on this computer.
  - Clicking a step takes you there and folds the checklist out of the way. The folded bar keeps
    the next step visible.
- **Scene editor:**
  - The stage toolbar fits on one line: the fold control is an icon button with a tooltip and
    screen-reader label, and toolbar gaps are tighter.
  - The quick-action bar's scene-theme selector is compact and matches the button height.
- **Assets:** the folder tool buttons always fit on one row; one button used to wrap onto its own
  line.
- **Hierarchy and consistency:**
  - Main sidebar: an accent bar marks the active view.
  - Top bar: the project name is separated from the logo.
  - View headers: one height, padding, background and title style in every view.
  - Section titles, the inspector header bar and the status bar use the same spacing rhythm.
- **Buttons and fields:**
  - Icons and text share one baseline, with consistent gaps in small buttons.
  - Visible keyboard focus rings on buttons, inputs and selects.
  - A compact `select.sm` for toolbars.
- Cards highlight subtly on hover.

## Fixed
- The onboarding checklist covered the bottom of the properties panel (e.g. choice option fields)
  with no way to keep it folded between sessions.
- The stage toolbar wrapped, leaving the fold control alone on a second line.
- The Assets folder toolbar wrapped one button onto its own line.
